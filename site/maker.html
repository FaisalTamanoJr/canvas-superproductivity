Canvas → Super Productivity

Make the Canvas Assignment Sync plugin for your school. It copies your Canvas assignments into Super Productivity as tasks, with deadlines and reminders.

1 Copy your Calendar Feed link

  1. Open Canvas and click Calendar in the left menu.
  2. Click Calendar Feed, at the bottom of the right side.
  3. Copy the link it shows.

2 Make your plugin

Your Canvas address (like canvas.myschool.edu) works too.

This page runs entirely in your browser. Your link is not sent or saved: only the address of your school's Canvas goes into the plugin file.

3 Install it

  1. Open the Super Productivity desktop app and go to Settings → Plugins.
  2. Under Install Plugin, click Choose Plugin File, pick the file you just downloaded, and confirm.
  3. Open Canvas Assignment Sync in the left menu, paste your Calendar Feed link again, and click Save link & sync.
